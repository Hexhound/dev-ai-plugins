Invoke the **promo-video** skill and run it for the app at `$ARGUMENTS` (ask if empty).

Work through its phases in order, pausing for the user at these points only:

1. After the brief — confirm audience, message, CTA and must-show features.
2. After music search — present 3–5 previews with one recommendation; the user picks or
   asks for another mood.
3. After the storyboard has passed the `promo-critic` review — show it with the critic's
   verdict and signature moments; get approval before capturing.
4. After the final render — deliver the file, the attribution text, and what wasn't
   verified.

Direct this promo from the product's own identity. Follow the playbook
(`references/craft.md`) strictly; never reuse another promo's look.

App project directory: $ARGUMENTS
