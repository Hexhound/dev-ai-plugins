# Chibi Chuu devlog: the chibis' glow-up

Narrator: Kokoro `af_heart` (Apache 2.0), speaking as "we". English. 1:38.
One line per voice clip (`id: text`); the edit is timed to these clips. A `|` marks where the
subtitles break a line (the voice doesn't say it); without one they break at commas.

## Intro (title card)
intro: Welcome back to Chibi Chuu! In this update, we rebuilt every chibi and every prop. Here's the before and after.

## The chibis: art and triangles
fast: Our first chibis were made fast, just to test the physics and the merging.
waste: They had around thirty thousand triangles each, but many were wasted, hidden inside the body or spent where nobody looks.
care: The new chibis were made with time and care, from many more animal references, so every chibi feels different.
eight: Eight thousand triangles each, placed where they show.
f1: On phones with Low detail on, they drop to two thousand five hundred. Our F1 test panel swaps them live.

## Textures and shading
flat: The old models had no textures, only flat colours and a toon light.
paint: The new ones have detailed, painted-style textures, and their expressions are drawn right over the painted eyes.

## Props
props: Every prop was rebuilt too, | with smoother, rounded edges and a new painted shader, | so the tank and the playground match the chibis.
tank: Even the tank got the painted look, with soft inked edges.

## Day and night
night: The day still turns from morning to night, and after dark, the chibis doze off and glow under the string lights.

## All seven modes
modes: And the new look is in all seven modes, from Classic to Hug Tower.

## Outro (end card)
outro: Thanks for watching! See you in the next update.
