Chibi Chuu devlog: the glow-up (before and after)

## Description

We rebuilt every chibi and every prop in Chibi Chuu. This devlog puts the old models (v1.0)
next to the new ones.

- The chibis: the first ones were made fast, just to test the physics and the merging. They
  had about 30,000 triangles each, and an x-ray shows how many surfaces were stacked inside
  the body. The new ones have 8,000 triangles placed where they show, and 2,500 on phones
  with Low detail on.
- Textures: the old models had flat colours. The new ones have painted-style textures, with
  their expressions drawn over the painted eyes.
- Props: the playground and the tank were rebuilt with rounded edges and a painted shader.
- Day and night, and all seven modes, before and after.
